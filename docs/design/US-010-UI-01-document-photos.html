US-010-UI-01 · Photos on a document row

Adding, viewing and deleting photos of a document the user has prepared (US-010-FE-01, Keisuke), on step detail and All documents. Pages are short stubs of the live StepDetailPage.tsx and DocumentsPage.tsx. Only the photo strip under each row, the full view and the delete confirm are new. Sample rows are Step 2's seeded documents. Limits are D26: JPG, PNG or WebP, 5 MB, 5 photos per document, Google users only.
