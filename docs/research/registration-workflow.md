## Notes

### Step 1A: Territorial scope

The Territorial Scope refers to the extent of the geographical area within which the pertinent business may locate its offices, stores, shops, branches, manufacturing or processing plants, or other business structures, or where the pertinent business name may be used without prejudice to engaging in business elsewhere. It is not to be considered as the geographical limit in which to transact business.

### Step 1B: SEC-eSPARC registration pathways

On the SEC-eSPARC portal, OneSEC (Zuper Easy Registration Online / ZERO) and Regular Processing are the two registration pathways.

| | OneSEC (ZERO) | Regular Processing |
|---|---|---|
| Application | 1-Day Approval for Stock and Non-Stock Corporations | Flexible structure (accommodates complex/specialized businesses) |
| Turnaround time | 1 working day (instant upon online payment) | 5 to 7 working days (requires manual SEC officer review) |
| Eligible entity types | Domestic Stock Corporations ONLY (including OPCs and corporations with 2 to 15 incorporators) | All entity types (Non-Stock, Partnerships, Foreign Corporations, Licensing, Stock) |
| Articles & By-Laws | Pre-formatted / standardized (cannot edit clauses or purposes outside predefined selections) | Customizable (supports custom primary/secondary purposes and custom corporate clauses) |
| Document signatures | Digital authentication via eSECURE / eSAP (100% paperless) | Physical notarization (must upload signed/notarized PDF copies) |

### DTI vs SEC

| | DTI | SEC |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Business name registration for sole proprietors | Registration for partnerships and corporations |
| Ownership | One individual owner | Multiple owners or a corporate structure |
| Legal entity | One-person business operating under a business name | Separate entity structure, shared ownership, governance, or growth plans |
| Liability | Unlimited personal liability for business debts | Limited liability protected by the corporate structure |
| Business name restriction | Cannot use "Corporation", "Company", "Incorporated", or "Inc." | Exclusively grants rights to use "Corp", "Inc.", "Corporation", or "Company" |
| Process | Simplicity and faster initial setup | Structure, ownership rules, continuity, and scale |

### Step 10A: BIR registration, Sole Proprietorship (Form 1901)

**Requirements**

- BIR Form No. 1901: 2 original signed copies.
- Government ID: 1 photocopy of any valid government-issued ID showing the applicant's photo, full name, address, and birthdate (e.g., PhilID/ePhilID, Driver's License, Passport, UMID).
  - If ID does not show address: 1 photocopy of a proof of residence or business address under the applicant's name.
  - For PRC-regulated professionals: 1 photocopy of a valid PRC ID + government-issued ID showing residence address.
- Business Name Registration: 1 photocopy of DTI Certificate of Business Name Registration (if operating under a trade name).
- Local Government Unit (LGU) Clearance: 1 photocopy of Mayor's / Business Permit (or Barangay Clearance depending on local RDO counter rules).
- Invoicing option document (choose one):
  - Option A: Buy BIR Printed Invoices (BPI) directly at the RDO counter.
  - Option B: Submit 1 original final clear sample/layout of your own custom primary invoices/receipts for printer accreditation.
- Payment: ₱30.00 Loose Documentary Stamp Tax (DST) to be affixed to the Certificate of Registration (BIR Form 2303).

**Other requirements**

- If transacting through a representative: 1 original Special Power of Attorney (SPA) executed by the applicant specifying the transaction; 1 photocopy of a valid government ID of both the taxpayer and representative with specimen signatures.
- Foreign nationals: 1 photocopy of 9(g) Work Visa or Alien Employment Permit (AEP).
- BMBE entities: 1 photocopy of DTI Certificate of Authority (Barangay Micro Business Enterprise).
- Tax-exempt / incentivized entities: 1 photocopy of Proof of Registration/Permit to Operate (e.g., BOI, PEZA, TIEZA, SBMA).
- Regulated transport (common carriers): 1 photocopy of franchise documents (e.g., LTFRB Certificate of Public Convenience).

### Step 10B: BIR registration, Corporations and Partnerships (Form 1903)

**Requirements**

- BIR Form No. 1903: 2 original copies signed by an authorized corporate officer.
- Entity registration certificate: 1 photocopy of SEC Certificate of Incorporation (for Corporations/OPCs), Certificate of Recording (for Partnerships), or License to Do Business in the Philippines (for Foreign Corporations).
- Constitutive documents: 1 photocopy of Articles of Incorporation (AOI) & By-Laws (for Corporations/OPCs) or Articles of Partnership.
- LGU Clearance: 1 photocopy of Mayor's / Business Permit under the registered corporate name.
- Proof of business address: 1 photocopy of Lease Contract or Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) under the company name.
- Invoicing option document (choose one):
  - Option A: Buy BIR Printed Invoices (BPI) directly at the RDO counter.
  - Option B: Submit 1 original final clear sample/layout of company-designed principal receipts/invoices.
- Payment: ₱30.00 Loose Documentary Stamp Tax (DST) to be affixed to BIR Form 2303.

**Additional requirements**

- If transacting through a representative: 1 original SPA executed by the applicant specifying the transaction; 1 photocopy of a valid government ID of both the taxpayer and representative with specimen signatures.
- Foreign nationals: 1 photocopy of 9(g) Work Visa or Alien Employment Permit (AEP).
- BMBE entities: 1 photocopy of DTI Certificate of Authority (Barangay Micro Business Enterprise).
- Tax-exempt / incentivized entities: 1 photocopy of Proof of Registration/Permit to Operate (e.g., BOI, PEZA, TIEZA, SBMA).
- Regulated transport (common carriers): 1 photocopy of franchise documents (e.g., LTFRB Certificate of Public Convenience).
